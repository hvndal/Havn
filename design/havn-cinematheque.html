R1 / Brief

One object, shot like a film still.

Hävn stops looking like a utility and starts looking like a well-made thing photographed on a seamless backdrop. The four-lid pill box becomes a clear glass capsule you read at a glance: what you have taken this week, what is left, and what you missed.

House rules

  • One object per screen.
  • Olive is the only colour.
  • Times, names and doses are set like film credits.
  • Cuts and dissolves. Nothing bounces.
  • Warm paper, never pure white.

Hävn keeps your medication schedule on this phone. There is no account and no internet permission.

Credit format

Time, name and dose, separated by spaced em dashes in Ink 40. Tabular figures so times line up down a list. No colons after labels, no bullets.

Technical labels

Screens carry a code: H-01 Today, H-02 Capsule, H-03 History, H-04 Reminders, H-05 New medication. Shelves are D-01 to D-07, Monday to Sunday. Pill forms are F-01 to F-06 and materials M-01 to M-06. Codes are always paired with a plain word.

R5 / Light

Light, in four passes.

Each pass is a small AGSL shader for Android 13 and up (RuntimeShader), with a bitmap fallback for Android 8 to 12. The previews here run the same maths in WebGL. Sources live in design/shaders/.

0.14

S-01 Grain

Every screen

Film grain refreshed at 24 fps, drawn over the whole screen with Overlay blending. The left half of the preview is clean. Use 0.10 on Matinée and 0.14 on Late show; the slider goes higher so you can see it.

Fallback
grain_256.png as a repeating ImageShader, offset randomly each 1/24 s
Cost
One hash per pixel
AGSL source — grain.agsl

S-02 Sweep

Today, Capsule, Dose

The seamless paper backdrop behind the capsule: wall colour easing into the cove and out to the floor, a soft pool of light and a gentle falloff at the corners. The pool drifts here to show the parameter; in the app it stays put.

Fallback
A vertical Brush.verticalGradient of the three sweep colours plus a radial pool
Cost
Trivial
AGSL source — sweep.agsl

S-03 Glint

Capsule views

A slow band of studio light that travels across the glass and only lives inside the capsule's silhouette. Drive it from scroll on Today and from a 26 second loop on Capsule. Never from anything fast.

Fallback
None. The glass layers already carry their highlights.
Cost
Two texture reads per pixel, inside the capsule bounds only
AGSL source — glint.agsl

S-04 Leak

Title card, end card

A warm light leak that blooms and dies during one dissolve: the first-launch title card into Today, and the end card when the day's last dose is taken. Twice a day at most, so it stays special.

Fallback
Plain 1200 ms dissolve
Cost
One texture read, only while the dissolve runs
AGSL source — leak.agsl

R7 / Assets

Specimens, glass and grain.

Everything here is rendered by the same model as the capsule, so the app's bitmaps match the design exactly. design/tools/export-assets.mjs writes them to design/assets/ at any size.

Pill forms

Capsule layers, Matinée

Glass is exported as a pair per layer: a tint (multiply; white is clear) and a light (add; black is nothing). That keeps the bright glints that an alpha matte would dim. Draw order: sweep, shadow, back tint, back light, ghost rings, pills, front tint, front light.

R8 / Frames

Eleven frames.

Each screen reads like a magazine page or a title card: one object, one statement, the schedule set as credits. Shown at 360 × 760 dp with the example schedule, Wednesday 14 October at 13:00. F01 and F09 are Late show; the rest are Matinée. F00 is the opening: every time the app opens, the next time and what is left are set enormous, hold, then the type dissolves down into Today (M-07).

R9 / Build

For the build.

The order to implement this in the Android app. Each step ships on its own and keeps the app working. design/README.md has the same steps with full Kotlin.

  1. 01

    Tokens

    Replace the palette in ui/theme/HavnColors.kt with Paper, Sweep, Ink, Rule, Olive (Matinée) and Screen, Velvet, Bone, Olive lift (Late show). Map the semantic tokens in HavnTokens.kt to them. Drop Clay, Amber, Slate and Sand as UI colours; keep them only as pill materials.

  2. 02

    Type

    In HavnTypography.kt point every style at HankenGrotesk, add credit and label styles with fontFeatureSettings = "tnum", and delete instrument_serif*.ttf. Sizes are in R4.

  3. 03

    Motion

    In HavnMotion.kt replace the springs with Projector and Dolly easings and the durations in R6. Remove every slide transition and celebrate(). Tabs cut.

  4. 04

    Assets

    Run node design/tools/export-assets.mjs, then copy design/assets/ into res/drawable-nodpi/ and capsule_layout.json into src/main/assets/. Add the icons as vector drawables.

  5. 05

    Capsule

    Add HavnCapsuleView.kt to replace HavnOrganizerView.kt. Draw order: sweep, shadow, back tint (Multiply), back light (Plus), olive rim and today mark, ghost rings, pills, front tint (Multiply), front light (Plus), glint. The view needs a week of doses, so add a week query to the repository and a CapsuleWeek model: seven shelves, each a list of doses with form, material and state.

  6. 06

    Shaders

    Load the four AGSL files from res/raw into RuntimeShader on API 33 and up. Below 33 use the fallbacks listed in R5. Pause grain and glint when the screen is not visible or the battery saver is on.

  7. 07

    Screens

    Rebuild Onboarding (F01), Today (F02, F09), Capsule (F03, F04), Dose (F05), Progress as History (F06), Reminders (F07) and Add medication (F08) to the frames. The tab bar is four text labels with an olive marker.

  8. 08

    Widget

    Rebuild HavnWidget as the 4 × 1 ticket stub and a 2 × 2 capsule tile (F10). Glance cannot run shaders, so the tile uses a pre-rendered capsule bitmap.

  9. 09

    Check

    Extend LaunchSmokeTest so it renders Today, Capsule and Dose in both themes, and compare them side by side with F02 to F09.
