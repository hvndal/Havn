Sounds

Two sets to compare by ear, both in the same key so either can be used. Turn your volume up a little: they are meant to be quiet. The phone above uses whichever set is chosen under it.

Sound rules

  • Sounds follow the Ring/Silent switch and mix with music (the iOS “ambient” audio setting). Silent means silent.
  • Only meaningful moments make a sound: logging, Undo, the ring closing, switches. Scrolling, tabs and typing stay quiet.
  • Every sound has a matching haptic, so the moment still lands with the sound off.
  • The reminder is one clear, gentle phrase under 1.5 seconds that doesn’t sound like any iPhone system sound.
  • A Sounds switch in Settings turns all of it off.

Pills that look like your pills

Each medication gets a real shape and colours, the way Apple Health lets you set them, drawn flat with one small highlight so it still feels like iOS. The background tint is the colour the medication adds to the Day Ring.
