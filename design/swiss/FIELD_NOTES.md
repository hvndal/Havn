# Field notes

One line shown under "That's everything." once all of today's doses are logged.
Rotates by day of year (`dayOfYear % size`). Ships inside the app, so it works offline.

Rules: habits and routine only. Never about a specific drug, dose, interaction or symptom.
No exclamation marks, no "you've got this". Set in IBM Plex Mono, uppercase via style, not in the strings.

Label: `FIELD NOTE · {n}`

## Routine
1. Keep pills next to something you already do daily.
2. A glass of water by the bed is a reminder you don't have to set.
3. Same time, same place. Habit does the remembering.
4. Mornings are easier when the box is out before the kettle is on.
5. Attach a dose to a meal you never skip.
6. If a time keeps getting missed, move the time, not the blame.
7. A reminder you snooze three times is a reminder at the wrong hour.
8. Weekends move. Your times can too.
9. Fewer decisions, fewer misses. Set it once.
10. The best schedule is the one you'd keep on a bad day.

## Supplies
11. Refill when you open the last strip, not the last pill.
12. Count once a week. Surprises are for birthdays.
13. Keep the leaflet. It answers more than search does.
14. Check expiry dates when you refill. It takes ten seconds.
15. Store them where you'll see them, out of reach of children.
16. Bathrooms are humid. A drawer elsewhere is often better.
17. Keep a few days' supply in your bag.

## Travel and days off
18. Travelling? Times follow your phone's clock.
19. Crossing time zones? Ask your pharmacist how to shift doses.
20. Pack doses in your carry-on, not your checked bag.
21. Bring the original box when you fly.
22. Holidays break routines. Keep one anchor.
23. A late night is not a reason to skip a log.

## Logging
24. Logging a skip is still logging.
25. A missed dose is a data point, not a verdict.
26. Honest history beats a perfect one.
27. The grid only remembers what you tell it.
28. Took it but forgot to tap? Tap now. The time can be corrected.
29. Two seconds a dose. That's the whole job.
30. Nothing here is shared. Write it as it happened.

## Doctor and pharmacy
31. Bring your history to appointments. It's on your phone.
32. Write questions down before the appointment, not after.
33. Your pharmacist is free to ask.
34. Tell every prescriber what you take, including vitamins.
35. Changes to your medication are your doctor's call. Logging is yours.
36. If something feels off, say so. Your log helps you describe it.

## Calm
37. Same as yesterday. That's the point.
38. Quiet days are good days.
39. Done is a full sentence.
40. The app is meant to take seconds, not attention.
41. Close it. It'll be here at the next dose.
42. Nothing until the next one.
43. Small, boring, repeated. That's how it works.
44. Today counted.
45. You were here. That's the log.

## Courses (show only when a course is active)
46. Each tick is a day closer to the last one.
47. Finish the course, even when you feel better, unless your doctor says otherwise.
48. When a course ends, Hävn will ask if you want to remove it.

## Seasonal (optional, by month)
49. January: new routines start with old anchors.
50. Summer: heat can affect storage. Keep pills somewhere cool.
51. Clocks change this month. Check your times the morning after.
52. December: plan refills before the holidays close the pharmacy.

## Colophon (rare, roughly 1 in 30 days)
53. Set in Archivo and IBM Plex Mono.
54. No account. No network. Still.
55. Made to be closed.
