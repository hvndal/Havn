# Hävn for iPhone: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026 (draft, to be published before the first iOS release)_

Hävn is made by Mander (https://mander.tech). This policy explains what the iPhone app does with your information.

## What we collect

Nothing. Hävn has no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app contains no code that sends your information over the internet, and no third-party software that could.

## What stays on your iPhone

The medications, schedules, people and dose history you enter are stored only inside Hävn's own storage on your iPhone. Hävn's widgets read the same information from a storage area that only Hävn and its widgets can open. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Hävn does not sync through iCloud.

## iPhone backups

By default, Hävn's information is left out of your iPhone backups. If you turn on **Include in iPhone Backups** in Settings, it is included in the backups your iPhone makes, in iCloud or on your computer, which Apple or your computer looks after.

## Files you export

**Export** in Settings creates a file and hands it to the place you choose, such as Files or another app. What happens to that file after that is up to you and that app.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, Hävn **reads** your medication list from Apple Health so you don't have to type it in. Hävn never writes to Health. Information from Health is used only inside the app, never for advertising, and is never shared. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.

## Reminders and alarms

Reminders are notifications your iPhone schedules on the device. By default they say that a dose is due without naming the medication. Names appear on the Lock Screen only if you turn on **Show Names on Lock Screen**.

If you choose an alarm for a medication, Hävn asks iOS to schedule it. The alarm shows the medication's name, because you chose it for that one.

## Face ID

If you turn on **Lock with Face ID**, iOS checks your face and tells Hävn only whether it matched. Hävn never sees or stores your face data.

## Deleting your data

Use **Erase All Data** in Settings, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes everything Hävn stored on the iPhone.

## Links

Links to mander.tech and to this policy open in your browser. Websites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

Hävn is not directed at children under 13.

## Medical disclaimer

Hävn is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions, and check with them before changing how you take a medication.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date. Hävn will never start collecting information without updating this policy and the app's App Store privacy details first.
