I-Hävn iyisikhumbuzo semithi esizolile futhi esiyimfihlo.

• Ukubuka kwanamuhla: bona umthamo ngamunye uhlelwe ekuseni, ntambama, kusihlwa nasebusuku
• Ishalofu lemithi: nsuku zonke, umjikelezo we-21/7, ukunciphisa kancane, ngamahora nalapho kudingeka
• Izikhumbuzo ezino-«Phawula njengokuphuzwe» no-«Ngemizuzu engu-15» khona kanye esazisweni
• Umlando: izinsuku ezingu-7/30/90, izitembu zenyanga, ikhadi le-PNG nombiko kadokotela we-PDF A4
• Abantu: amaphrofayela angu-2 mahhala efonini eyodwa (i-Supporter ivula abanye abaningi)
• Itimu ekhanyayo nemnyama
• Iwijethi yesikrini sasekhaya
• Izingqikithi zokwenza ikhophi ye-.havn ebethelwe ngephasikhodi ngaphansi kokulawula kwakho

Iyimfihlo ngokwemvelo: ayikho i-akhawunti, azikho iziphakeli, akukho ukulandela umkhondo futhi akukho ukulayisha efwini. Idatha ihlala efonini.

Izikhumbuzo eziyisisekelo zihlala zimahhala phakade futhi azinazo izikhangiso. Ukuthenga kanye okungakhethwa kwe-Supporter kuvula amaphrofayela engeziwe.

Yakhiwe ngu-Mander — mander.tech — ukwakhiwa kwamawebhusayithi, izinhlelo zokusebenza namaphrojekthi.

I-Hävn iyithuluzi lokukhumbuza futhi ayinikezi izeluleko zezokwelapha. Landela njalo imiyalelo kadokotela noma yosokhemisi wakho.
